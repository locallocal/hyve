# Hyve Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 10, 2026**

**Effective date: August 10, 2026**

Hyve is a local-first AI assistant client. This Policy is provided by the Hyve developer or operator identified on the page from which the application is distributed ("we," "us," or "our"). It explains how Hyve handles information relating to you and how you can control that information.

## Key points

- **No Hyve account is required:** The current version does not require you to register or sign in, and it does not collect an account password.
- **Local storage by default:** Your profile, agent configuration, conversations, and most feature data are stored on your device by default.
- **You choose where requests go:** When you use cloud AI, remote MCP, a custom endpoint, or another network feature, necessary data is sent directly from your device to the third party you selected.
- **No advertising profiles:** The current version does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or crash-reporting services, and we do not sell your personal information.
- **Permissions are used on demand:** Camera, photo, media, or file access is requested only when you actively use the relevant feature.

Please read this Policy in full. We provide additional notices for sensitive permissions or processing that requires separate consent. If you do not agree to optional processing, you can choose not to enable the relevant feature.

## 1. Scope and processing roles

This Policy applies to the Hyve application itself. Third-party AI providers, MCP servers, Skill sources, custom endpoints, external links, and operating-system sharing or file-selection services may process information as independent controllers or businesses under their own privacy policies.

Hyve generally acts as a local client that processes data according to your instructions. You decide which service to connect, what content to submit, and whether to approve a tool call. Do not send a service information that it should not receive.

## 2. Information Hyve processes

### 2.1 Information stored on your device

To provide its features, Hyve may process and store the following on your device:

- **Profile and preferences:** The display name, avatar, language, theme, font size, and interface preferences you set;
- **Agent and service configuration:** Agent names and avatars, model provider, model, endpoint, system prompt, parameters, MCP server configuration, and Skill settings;
- **Credentials:** AI service API keys and MCP access tokens or local-service environment variables that you provide;
- **Conversation content:** Your input, AI responses, reasoning content, summaries or memory, attachments, generated files, and local file paths;
- **Execution and usage records:** Tool calls, command executions, file edits, Skill activation states, error states, durations, and token usage; and
- **Security and configuration records:** Local records relating to Skill installation, signatures, permissions, policies, and audits.

The current version does not collect a phone number, email address, or password to create a Hyve account. We do not request your legal identity unless you voluntarily include it in a prompt, file, configuration, or feedback message.

### 2.2 Information sent to services you select

When you actively use a relevant feature, the following may be sent directly to a service you select or configure:

- **AI model services:** Prompts, conversation history or summaries needed for context, system instructions, selected attachments, tool definitions and results, model selection, and generation parameters;
- **MCP or other tool services:** Server connection information, tool names, call arguments, necessary conversation content, and tool results;
- **Custom endpoints or local models:** Request content and authentication information required by the endpoint you configured;
- **Skill or resource sources:** The requested repository, directory, archive, or catalog information when installing or updating a Skill; and
- **System features:** Files or text you actively share, export, save, or open are provided to the system app or destination app you choose.

A third-party service may also automatically receive your IP address, request time, and ordinary device or network connection information. The third party determines its collection, retention, model-training use, international transfers, and deletion procedures. Review its policy before use.

### 2.3 Device permissions

- **Camera:** Used only when you choose to take a photo for an attachment;
- **Photos or media:** Used only when you select an image or video, or save generated content;
- **Files:** Used only when you use the system picker to select an attachment or Skill package, or save a file; and
- **Network:** Used to connect to AI or MCP services, custom endpoints, Skill sources, and external links that you select.

You can withdraw permissions in your operating-system settings. The related feature may stop working, but features that do not require the permission will remain available. The current version does not request precise location, contacts, or microphone access.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

We process information only as needed for defined features, including to:

- save and restore local configuration, conversations, and preferences;
- send requests to selected services and display results according to your instructions;
- perform tool, MCP, and Skill operations you approve or configure;
- calculate local token usage, manage context, diagnose local errors, and maintain data integrity;
- protect credentials, packages, tool execution, and local data; and
- comply with applicable law or protect the lawful interests of you, others, and the application.

Depending on applicable law, the legal basis may be performance of a contract with you, your consent, compliance with a legal obligation, or another permitted basis. We request permission or separate consent where required for optional permissions or sensitive personal information.

## 4. Sharing, service providers, and international transfers

1. We do not sell or rent your personal information or use local conversations for advertising profiles.

2. We do not otherwise provide personal information to another party except for services you actively select, when required by law, to protect vital lawful interests, or as part of a lawful corporate reorganization.

3. A provider or custom endpoint you select may be located outside your country or region. Whether an international transfer occurs depends on the endpoint and the provider's arrangements. Before sending personal information abroad, confirm that you are authorized to do so and review the provider's data locations, transfer mechanisms, and privacy terms.

4. If we later introduce first-party cloud sync, telemetry, advertising, or another service that changes the data flows described here, we will update this Policy before enabling it and provide any notice or consent required by law.

## 5. Storage and retention

1. The local database, conversation attachments, generated files, Skills, and summary files are generally stored in the application's data directory until you delete the relevant content, clear app data, or uninstall the application.

2. AI API keys are encrypted before being written to the local database. Encryption keys and MCP credentials are stored using secure storage provided by the operating system. Conversation text and ordinary attachments are generally not separately encrypted by Hyve and instead rely on device, operating-system, and file-system protections.

3. Content you export, share, save to a system gallery, or copy elsewhere is no longer controlled by deletion inside Hyve. Copies may also remain in operating-system or user-enabled device backups.

4. A third party retains information under its own policy. Deleting a local Hyve record does not automatically delete data already received by a third party. Use that party's deletion controls or contact it directly.

## 6. Your rights and controls

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to know about, access, copy, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or withdraw consent for processing. You can:

- edit local profile information and preferences in the profile or settings pages;
- clear or delete conversations and delete agents, MCP servers, or installed Skills;
- remove API keys, access tokens, custom endpoints, or other connection settings;
- withdraw camera, photo, media, or file permissions in system settings;
- clear application data or uninstall Hyve to delete on-device app data; and
- submit a privacy request using the developer contact details on the application's distribution page.

To protect data, we may need to verify the requester's relationship to the relevant device or data. Exercise rights relating to information held by a third party directly with that party.

## 7. Security and your responsibilities

We use measures such as local storage, operating-system secure storage, API-key encryption, permission prompts, tool risk classifications, Skill validation, and sandboxing where available. No storage, transmission, or software system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Protect your device with an access code and current security updates, do not share API keys, and do not connect unknown MCP endpoints or install untrusted Skills. Before submitting content to AI, remove unnecessary identity documents, financial-account details, health information, precise location, children's data, trade secrets, and other sensitive information.

## 8. Children's information

Hyve is not directed to children below the minimum age for independent consent in their jurisdiction. A minor should use Hyve only with guidance from a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian should carefully decide whether a feature is appropriate and avoid sending a child's information to third-party models or tools.

If you believe a child's information has been handled inappropriately, contact us using the method below. We will assist with data within our control as required by law.

## 9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as features, third-party services, data practices, or legal requirements change, and will update the date above. We will provide reasonable notice of changes that materially affect your rights through the app, release notes, or distribution page. Where consent is required, we will request it before the new processing begins.

## 10. Contact us

For questions, comments, or requests about this Policy or personal-information processing, use the developer contact details shown on the application's distribution page. You may also use a feedback channel if your distribution of Hyve expressly provides an active one.
